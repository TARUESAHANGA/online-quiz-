How to play the Quiz

  1. You will have only 15 seconds per question.
  2. You can choose the category of questions you desire to answer from.
  3. Once you select your answer, you can't reselect.
  4. You can't select any option once time goes off.
  5. The Quiz got different types of questions: multiple-choice, true/false and fill in the blank.
  6. You will receive a summary of your performance at the of the quiz.
